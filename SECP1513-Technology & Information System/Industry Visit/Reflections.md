
# Industry Visit to Johor Corporation (JCorp)

> **Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM)**  
> Semester 1, 2025/2026

---

## 📋Details

On 7th January 2026, us from section 02 of Technology & Informations System taught by Dr. Aryati had the golden opportunity to do an industry visit to Johor Corporation or JCorp for short. This opulent event is orchestrated specially for us from the second section to expose us to the real-life industry as soon as possible.

---

## 📖 Preparations

Before the promised day come, I have to do some preparations to avoid looking clueless and ignorant. Therefore, I did some research and digging into everything about JCorp, starting from their history, foundation and the technologies they used. My objective is to be fully informed and avoid any appearance of unpreparedness.

---

## 🎯 Learning Experience / Reflections

The experience of visiting JCorp's offices worth more than gold, providing me valuable insights into JCorp's history, technologies and business plans.

I learned a lot of important things today, such as how artificial intelligence and cloud and be used to cut the cost of managing a locally based server. The presenter, Sir Nizam, shared with us about JCorp's step-by-step plan to change from local server to cloud.

Other than that, we also learned that soft skills are really important to work in JCorp or in a company of similar league, proving that communications is just as important as individual intelligence.
